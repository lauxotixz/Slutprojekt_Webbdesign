Help & Support

Need help with your order, delivery, or care instructions? Here are the most common questions we receive about our knives and shopping experience.

If you run into any problems, please reach us at kuroha@gmail.com.

How do I choose the right knife?

Start with the type of cooking you do most often. Our collection includes versatile options for everyday use, as well as more specialized knives for detailed work. If you usually prepare vegetables, meat, or sauces, we recommend choosing a knife that matches your main tasks and preferred grip.

For beginners, a balanced all-purpose knife is often the best place to start. If you already know your preferred style, you can choose a more specific design for better control and comfort during longer sessions in the kitchen.

How should I care for my knife?

Clean your knife after use, dry it immediately, and store it safely. Avoid soaking it in water or placing it in the dishwasher to preserve the finish and edge over time.

Use a soft cloth for drying and store the blade in a safe place when not in use. Regular care helps maintain both the appearance and performance of the knife, especially if you use it often.

Do you offer support after purchase?

Yes. If you have questions about product care, sizing, or delivery, please contact us and we will be happy to help. We are here to support you before and after your purchase so you can feel confident in your choice.

Our team can also guide you through general product questions, handling advice, and recommendations based on your cooking needs.

What should I expect from delivery?

Orders are prepared carefully and packed to protect the product during shipping. We aim to make the process simple, secure, and easy to follow from checkout to delivery.

If you have questions about timing or the status of an order, feel free to contact us and we will help you with the next steps.
